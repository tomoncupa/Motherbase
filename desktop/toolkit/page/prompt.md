PATTERNS
A pattern is any `note` row whose text contains a tag `#p-<name>`
(for example `#p-scroll`, `#p-snack`, `#p-avoid`). Group them by name.
Window: the last 28 days, unless I give another.

FOR EACH TIME IT HAPPENED, FILL IN:
1. When: date, weekday, clock time (payload `t`, else the time typed in the line).
2. Felt before: the line's own `mood` and `feel`; else the nearest earlier
   `note` with a mood that day, and how many minutes before it was.
3. Body: last night's sleep and today's weigh-in if there are `ev` rows for
   them (read `field` rows to find what I track); hours since the last `meal`
   row; trained that day or not (`session` and `set` rows).
4. Load: `tick` rows done against the day's `plan`; any `spend` that day; at
   the desk or not at that minute (`deskon`), and the day's top `screen` programs.
5. After: the next `note` within 3 hours that carries a mood or feel.
   Mood after minus mood before is the payoff signal.
6. Optional words on the line, counted when present: `away` or `toward`,
   and a mode word (`critic`, `demanding`, `soother`, `detached`, `child`).

THEN, PER PATTERN:
- Count, times per week, and the spread across the day in 4-hour blocks.
- Cues: compare days it happened with days it did not, in the same window.
  For each candidate (short sleep, no training, long gap since a meal, low
  mood, missed ticks, weekday, a time block), give the rate on days it
  happened, the rate on other days, and the ratio. Rank by ratio. Mark any
  with fewer than 5 times behind it as TOO FEW, and never rank on it.
- Payoff: average mood change from before to after, and the feel words before
  and after. Name the payoff as a best guess (relief, avoidance, stimulation,
  comfort, control, connection), with the numbers behind it.
- Gaps: name every field above you could not fill and why. Who I was with,
  and where I was away from the desk, are never in the rows; say so once.

CAPABILITY, OPPORTUNITY, MOTIVATION (Michie 2011), per pattern, from the
numbers above only:
- Capability: on days it happened, is there a replacement I have shown I can
  do (a `toward` line, a tick, a set)? If not, the gap is capability.
- Opportunity: does it cluster in one place, time or program (`deskon`,
  `screen`, a time block)? Then the cue is in my surroundings.
- Motivation: does mood rise after (an automatic payoff), or does it follow a
  `critic` or `demanding` mode line (a deeper, schema-level driver)?
Name the weakest of the three, then pick at most 3 techniques from the
Behaviour Change Technique Taxonomy, version 1, that target it, by number
and name (for example 12.3 Avoiding cues, 8.2 Behaviour substitution,
1.4 Action planning, 8.4 Habit reversal, 4.2 Information about what comes

  One step from where I am, never the finished version.
- Compare with the previous 28 days if the pattern shows up there: up, down
