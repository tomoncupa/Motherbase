Start here

Your phone first, then your computer.

On your phone

1Open STATUS

Open STATUS tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/status/

iPhone: open it in Safari. Android: open it in Chrome.

2Add it to your home screen

iPhone: tap the share button at the bottom, the square with an arrow out of it. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, tap Add.

Android: tap the three dots at the top right, tap Add to Home screen, tap Add.

Open STATUS from that icon from now on.

3Set up STATUS

  1. Open STATUS from the icon.
  2. Tap the gear at the top right, then TRACK.
  3. Tap Weight to switch it from pounds to kilograms.
  4. Tap any other name to change it. Under ADD, tap anything else you want to track.
  5. Tap DONE.
  6. On Today, tap a tile to log it: weight, sleep, steps, mood, energy.
On your computer

4Make a Google Sheet

  1. Go to sheets.new.
  2. Name it, for example My tracking.
  3. Leave it open.

5Open MAIN MENU

Open MAIN MENU tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/

6Make your sync code

  1. Click DATA at the top right, then Copy the Apps Script.
  2. In your sheet, click Extensions, then Apps Script.
  3. Select everything in the editor, delete it, paste, and save.
  4. Click Deploy, then New deployment.
  5. Click the gear beside Select type, choose Web app.
  6. Execute as Me. Who has access Anyone. Click Deploy.
  7. Click Authorize access, pick your Google account, then Advanced, Go to (your project), Allow.
  8. Copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec.
  9. Back in MAIN MENU under DATA, paste it into the box under SYNC.
  10. Turn on Keep the sheet up to date by itself.
  11. Click Sync every app now.

7Put the same address on your phone

  1. Send the address to yourself in Telegram, Saved Messages.
  2. Copy it on your phone.
  3. Open STATUS from the icon, tap the gear, then DATA.
  4. Scroll to GOOGLE SHEET MIRROR and paste it into the box.
  5. Turn on Push at the end of each day.
  6. Tap Save & sync.
Then these two

8TRAIN, for your sessions

Open TRAIN tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/train/
  1. Add it to your home screen, the same as step 2, and open it from the icon.
  2. Tap Start New Session.
  3. Pick an exercise, then put in your weight and reps for each set.

9CHECK IN, for your photos

Open CHECK IN tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/checkin/
  1. Add it to your home screen, the same as step 2, and open it from the icon.
  2. Tap Take photos and follow what it says.
  3. Fill in the weight and the questions under it.
  4. Tap Send to coach.

That is the setup. Anything else, ask.
