Start here

Your phone first, then your computer.

On your phone

1Open STATUS

Open STATUS tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/status/

iPhone: open it in Safari. Android: open it in Chrome.

2Add it to your home screen

iPhone: tap the share button at the bottom, the square with an arrow out of it. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, tap Add.

Android: tap the three dots at the top right, tap Add to Home screen, tap Add.

From now on, open STATUS from that icon.

3Set up STATUS

  1. Open STATUS from the icon.
  2. Tap TRACK. Turn on what you want to track, turn off what you do not.
  3. Weight starts in pounds. Tap Weight to change it to kilograms.
  4. Go back and log today.
On your computer

4Make a Google Sheet

  1. Go to sheets.new.
  2. Name it, for example My tracking.
  3. Leave it open.

5Open MAIN MENU

Open MAIN MENU tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/

6Make your sync code

  1. In MAIN MENU, click DATA, top right.
  2. Click Copy the Apps Script.
  3. In your sheet, click Extensions, then Apps Script.
  4. Select everything in the editor, delete it, paste, and save.
  5. Click Deploy, then New deployment.
  6. Click the gear beside Select type, choose Web app.
  7. Execute as Me. Who has access Anyone. Click Deploy.
  8. Click Authorize access, pick your Google account, then Advanced, Go to (your project), Allow.
  9. Copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec.
  10. In MAIN MENU under DATA, paste it into the box.
  11. Turn on Keep the sheet up to date by itself.
  12. Click Sync every app now.

7Put the same address on your phone

  1. Send the address to yourself in Telegram, Saved Messages.
  2. Copy it on your phone.
  3. Open STATUS from the icon, open DATA.
  4. Paste it into the box.
  5. Turn on Keep the sheet up to date by itself, then tap Sync now.
Then these two

8TRAIN, for your sessions

Open TRAIN tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/train/

Add it to your home screen, the same as step 2. Open it from the icon. It asks whether you use kilograms or pounds.

9CHECK IN, for your photos

Open CHECK IN tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/checkin/

Add it to your home screen, the same as step 2. Open it from the icon and take your first photo.

That is the setup. Anything else, ask.
