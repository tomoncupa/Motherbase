Part one: on your phoneSteps 1 to 4

1Open STATUS

Open STATUS tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/status/

iPhone: open it in Safari. Android: open it in Chrome.

2Add it to your home screen

iPhone: tap the share button at the bottom, the square with an arrow out of it. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, tap Add.

Android: tap the three dots at the top right, tap Add to Home screen, tap Add.

Open STATUS from that icon from now on.

Do not skip this step. A phone clears a website it has not seen in a week. Opened from the icon, it keeps your data.

3Sign in with Google

  1. Open STATUS from the icon.
  2. Tap the gear at the top right. That is Settings.
  3. Tap DATA.
  4. Under LIVE SYNC, tap Sign in with Google and pick your account.
  5. It says Live as and your email. Everything you log now joins your account.

Use this same Google account on every phone and computer.

4Set up STATUS

  1. In Settings, tap TRACK.
  2. Tap Weight to switch it from pounds to kilograms.
  3. Tap any other name to change it. Under ADD, tap anything else you want to track.
  4. Tap DONE.
  5. On Today, tap a tile to log it: weight, sleep, steps, mood, energy.
Part two: on your computerStep 5. Every app in one window, and STATUS in the corner of your screen

5Install Motherbase

On Windows:

Download Motherbase for Windows One file, Motherbase Setup.exe, about 1MB
  1. If your browser says the file is not commonly downloaded, choose Keep. In Edge it is under the three dots beside the download, then Show more, Keep anyway.
  2. Open the file.
  3. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. It says this for any program it has not seen many times before.
  4. It asks Have you used Motherbase before? Click Yes, I already use it, since your phone is signed in.
  5. Click Sign in and pick the same Google account as your phone.
  6. Pick a keyboard shortcut for writing an entry, and click through to Open Motherbase.

Motherbase and STATUS are now on your desktop and in the Start menu. To remove them later: Settings, Apps, Motherbase.

On a Mac, or a computer you cannot install on: open MAIN MENU in Chrome, click DATA at the top right, and click Sign in with Google under LIVE SYNC.

Sending me a screenshotThe only thing I see

How to send one

I cannot see your apps. When something looks wrong, or you are not sure what you are looking at, a picture of the screen answers it in one message.

iPhone: press the side button and the volume up button at the same time, quickly. The picture goes to your photos.

Android: press the power button and the volume down button at the same time. The picture goes to your photos.

Windows: press Windows + Shift + S, drag over the part of the screen, and paste it into the chat.

Send it to me in the same chat we use. Say what you were trying to do when it happened. Nothing is too small.

What I can and cannot see

I see your check ins, once you press Send to coach, and whatever you screenshot me. That is all. Your entries are in your own Google account and on your own devices, and neither one reaches me.

Optional: a copy in a Google Sheet

A spreadsheet of everything you log, in your own Google Drive. Do it once, on your computer.

Make the sheet

Make a blank Google Sheet sheets.new
  1. Click Untitled spreadsheet at the top left and name it, for example My tracking.
  2. Leave this tab open.

Copy the script

  1. In MAIN MENU, click DATA at the top right.
  2. Click Copy the Apps Sync Script. It is now on your clipboard.

Paste it into your sheet

  1. Go back to the Google Sheet tab.
  2. Click Extensions, then Apps Script. A new tab opens.
  3. Click Untitled project at the top left and name it My tracking sync.
  4. Click once anywhere in the code in the middle of the screen.
  5. Press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + V to paste over it, then Ctrl + S to save. On a Mac, Cmd instead of Ctrl.

Deploy it

  1. Click the blue Deploy button at the top right, then New deployment.
  2. Click the gear beside "Select type", then Web app.
  3. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone.
  4. Click Deploy.
  5. Click Authorize access and pick your Google account.
  6. It says Google hasn't verified this app. Click Advanced, then Go to My tracking sync (unsafe), then Allow. Google says this for any script it has not reviewed, your own included.

Link it

  1. Click Copy under Web app URL. It ends in /exec. Keep it to yourself: anyone with it can write to your sheet.
  2. In MAIN MENU, DATA, paste it into the box under SYNC.
  3. Turn on Keep the sheet up to date by itself.
  4. Click Sync every app now. Your sheet fills up.

One computer is enough. Your phone's entries reach it through your Google sign-in.
