Start here

Your phone first, then your computer. About ten minutes.

This is your own private tracker. Everything you log stays on your phone and in a Google Sheet that belongs to you. Nobody else can open it, not even me. The only things I ever see are the screenshots you send me and the check in you press send on.

Open this page on your computer too. Steps 4 to 7 are done there, and you will want to be able to tap these links rather than type them out.

This page, for your computer tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/guide.html

Email it to yourself, or send it to yourself in Telegram, Saved Messages.

Every blue box is a link. Tap it and it opens in a new tab, so these instructions stay open behind it. Anything in bold is a button or a switch to look for on the screen.

Your browser may not look exactly like the words here. Buttons move around between phones, between Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone, and between versions. If a name here is not where I say it is, it is on the same screen somewhere nearby. Send me a screenshot and I will point at it.

Part one: on your phoneSteps 1, 2 and 3

1Open STATUS

Open STATUS tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/status/

iPhone: open it in Safari. Android: open it in Chrome. It opens in a new tab, so this page stays where it is.

2Add it to your home screen

iPhone: tap the share button at the bottom, the square with an arrow out of it. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, tap Add.

Android: tap the three dots at the top right, tap Add to Home screen, tap Add.

Open STATUS from that icon from now on.

Android says Add to Home screen, not Install. That is expected, and the icon works the same. Install is a different thing Android offers only to apps that ship an install file, and these do not have one yet.

Do not skip this step. A phone clears a website it has not seen in a week. Opened from the icon, it keeps your data.

3Set up STATUS

  1. Open STATUS from the icon.
  2. Tap the gear at the top right. That is Settings.
  3. Tap TRACK.
  4. Tap Weight to switch it from pounds to kilograms.
  5. Tap any other name to change it. Under ADD, tap anything else you want to track.
  6. Tap DONE.
  7. On Today, tap a tile to log it: weight, sleep, steps, mood, energy.
Part two: on your computerSteps 4 to 7. This part is easier with a keyboard, and it is what gives you a backup

4Make a Google Sheet

Make a blank Google Sheet sheets.new
  1. It makes a new empty sheet and opens it. Sign in if it asks.
  2. Click Untitled spreadsheet at the top left and name it, for example My tracking.
  3. Leave this tab open. You come back to it in step 6.

5Open MAIN MENU

Open MAIN MENU tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/

6Make your personal sync code

This is the longest step. It is one instruction per line, so do them in order and do not skip one. It only happens once.

Copy the script

  1. In MAIN MENU, click DATA at the top right.
  2. Click Copy the Apps Sync Script. It is now on your clipboard.

Paste it into your sheet

  1. Go back to the Google Sheet tab you left open in step 4.
  2. Click Extensions in the menu at the top.
  3. Hover over the list until you see Apps Script, and click it. A new tab opens.
  4. At the top left it says Untitled project. Click that and name it My tracking sync. Do this now, because Google shows you this name again in a minute and Untitled project reads like something you did not mean to make.
  5. Click once anywhere in the code in the middle of the screen.
  6. Press Ctrl + A to select all of it. On a Mac, Cmd + A.
  7. Press Ctrl + V to paste over it. On a Mac, Cmd + V. Everything that was there is replaced.
  8. Press Ctrl + S to save. On a Mac, Cmd + S.

Deploy it

  1. Click the blue Deploy button at the top right.
  2. Click New deployment. A pop up window opens.
  3. Click the gear beside "Select type", then click Web app.
  4. In Description, type anything you like. It is only a note to yourself. Leave it empty if you want.
  5. Execute as: Me.
  6. Who has access: Anyone.
  7. Click Deploy.

Google will now warn you about your own script

What comes next looks alarming and is not. You wrote this script into your own account a minute ago, and Google shows this screen for any script it has not reviewed. It cannot be turned off. Nothing here is being sent to me: the sheet is yours, the script is yours, and it only writes to the sheet you just made.

  1. Click Authorize access.
  2. Pick your Google account.
  3. It says Google hasn't verified this app. Click Advanced at the bottom left.
  4. Click Go to My tracking sync (unsafe). That is the name you typed earlier, and the word unsafe is Google's, for any script it has not reviewed.
  5. Click Allow.
  6. Google emails you to say your script was given access to your account. That email is expected. You do not need to do anything with it.

Take the code

  1. A box shows a Web app URL. It ends in /exec.
  2. Click Copy. That is your personal sync code.
  3. Keep it to yourself. Anyone with that address can write to your sheet. Treat it like a password, and do not post it anywhere.
  4. Go back to the MAIN MENU tab, click DATA, and paste it into the box under SYNC.
  5. Turn on Keep the sheet up to date by itself.
  6. Click Sync every app now. Your sheet fills up. That is it working.

7Put your sync code on your phone

Your phone and your computer are two separate copies until they both hold the same code. This is what joins them.

  1. Send the code to yourself in Telegram, Saved Messages. Email or any chat with yourself works too.
  2. It has to be the exact code and nothing else: no spaces, no words around it, nothing cut off the end. It starts with https and ends with /exec.
  3. On your phone, copy it.
  4. Open STATUS from the icon.
  5. Tap the gear at the top right. That is Settings.
  6. Tap DATA.
  7. The sync box is at the top. Paste the code into it.
  8. Tap Save & sync.

Paste it once on a device and every app on that device has it.

Part three: the other two appsSteps 8 and 9, back on your phone

8TRAIN, for your sessions

How to send one

I cannot see your apps. When something looks wrong, or you are not sure what you are looking at, a picture of the screen answers it in one message.

iPhone: press the side button and the volume up button at the same time, quickly. The picture goes to your photos.

Android: press the power button and the volume down button at the same time. The picture goes to your photos.

Send it to me in the same chat we use. Say what you were trying to do when it happened. Nothing is too small.

What I can and cannot see

I see your check ins, once you press Send to coach, and whatever you screenshot me. That is all. Your sheet is in your own Google account, your tracking is on your own phone, and neither one reaches me.
