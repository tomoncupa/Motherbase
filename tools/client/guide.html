Start here

Three parts: your phone, then your computer, then two more apps. About twenty minutes, once.

This is a set of small apps for tracking training and daily life. There is no account, no password and no sign-up. You open a link and it works, with or without signal.

Everything you type is saved on your own device, not on somebody else's server. Part 2 sets up a Google Sheet that belongs to you, so your phone and your computer hold the same thing, and so a copy exists if you lose the phone.

What you need: your phone, a computer, a Google account, and a way to send yourself a link. Telegram Saved Messages works well, because it is on both devices.

Part 1 · Your phone

Get STATUS onto your home screen and log one day.

1Open STATUS on your phone

Open STATUS tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/status/

On an iPhone this has to be opened in Safari. On Android, use Chrome.

2Put it on your home screen, before you type anything

Do this first. Not because it looks nicer, but because phones delete websites they think you have stopped using. An iPhone clears a website's saved data after about a week of not visiting it. Adding it to your home screen tells the phone this is an app, and your data stays.

iPhone

  1. Tap the share button at the bottom of the screen. It is the square with an arrow pointing up.
  2. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Tap the three dots in the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.

From now on, open it from that icon. Not from the link, not from a bookmark. Close the browser tab you used, so you cannot end up typing into two different copies.

3Set up STATUS from the icon

Open STATUS from your new home screen icon. It arrives ready to track weight, sleep, steps, mood, energy, and whether you trained.

  1. Tap TRACK. Turn off anything you will not use, and turn on anything you want that is not there. You can change this any day.
  2. Weight starts in pounds. If you use kilograms, tap Weight in TRACK and change the unit.
  3. Go back and log today: your weight, last night's sleep, how you feel.

Forgot yesterday? Any past day can be filled in later. That is normal here, not a workaround.

Part 2 · Your computer

A Google Sheet of your own, so your phone and computer agree, and nothing lives in one place only.

4Make a blank Google Sheet

  1. On the computer, go to sheets.new. That makes one empty sheet.
  2. Give it a name, for example My tracking.
  3. Leave it open. You come back to it in step 6.

Do not type anything into it. The apps fill it in.

5Open MAIN MENU on the computer

Open MAIN MENU tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/

This is the home screen for all the apps. It will look empty, because this computer has none of your data yet. The next step fixes that.

6Make your sync code

This is the longest step, and you only ever do it once. It gives you a private web address that belongs to your sheet and to nobody else's.

  1. In MAIN MENU, click DATA in the top right.
  2. Click Copy the Apps Script. That copies a short piece of text.
  3. Go to your sheet. Click Extensions, then Apps Script.
  4. Select everything already in the editor, delete it, and paste what you copied. Save it with the disk icon.
  5. Click Deploy, then New deployment.
  6. Click the gear icon beside "Select type" and choose Web app.
  7. Set Execute as: Me, and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  8. Google asks for permission the first time. Click Authorize access, choose your Google account, then Advanced, then Go to (your project name), then Allow. Google shows that warning for any script it has not reviewed. It is warning you about your own script.
  9. Copy the Web app URL it gives you. It ends in /exec.
  10. Back in MAIN MENU, under DATA, paste that address into the box.
  11. Turn on Keep the sheet up to date by itself.
  12. Click Sync every app now. Your sheet fills with tabs.

Keep that address to yourself. "Who has access: Anyone" means anyone holding the exact address can write to your sheet. Nobody can guess it, and nobody is given it. Do not post it anywhere public.

7Put the same address on your phone

That address is what joins your phone and your computer, so both need it.

  1. On the computer, send the address to yourself. Telegram, Saved Messages is the easiest way.
  2. On the phone, copy it out of Telegram.
  3. Open STATUS from your home screen icon, then open DATA.
  4. Paste the address into the box.
  5. Turn on Keep the sheet up to date by itself, then tap Sync now.

What you logged on the phone in part 1 now shows up on the computer. You paste the address once per device, not once per app: every app on that device uses it.

Syncing needs signal. Logging never does. Log at the gym with no bars and it saves, then syncs later.

Part 3 · The other two apps

Same routine each time: open the link on the phone, add it to the home screen, use it from the icon.

8TRAIN, your training log

Open TRAIN tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/train/

Add it to your home screen the same way as step 2, then open it from the icon.

The first time it opens it asks whether you use kilograms or pounds. That is the only thing it cannot work out for itself. Everything else is ready: over a hundred exercises are already in there, and you can add your own.

It is built to be used one-handed, between sets, while you are at the gym.

9CHECK IN, your progress photos

Open CHECK IN tomoncupa.github.io/Mainmenu-client/checkin/

Add it to your home screen, the same as before.

  1. The first time, it downloads what it needs to measure a photo. That needs signal once. After that it works offline.
  2. Take your first photo. Prop the phone up, and it tells you where to stand.
  3. Your weight comes from STATUS. You never type it twice.
  4. When you are ready to send a check-in, it makes one file. Send that file to your coach.

The photos stay on your phone. Sending that file is the only time any of it leaves, and you choose when.

What each app is for

AppWhat it does
STATUSYour day: sleep, weight, steps, mood, food, spending and notes. Phone or computer.
TRAINTraining log. Exercises, sets, reps, records. Built for a phone.
CHECK INProgress photos, weight and a few questions, and a file to send your coach. Phone or computer.
BLOCKPlan a day as blocks and tick them off. For a computer.
QUESTSYour todo list. Type "call the gym tomorrow 3pm" and it reads the date for you. Phone or computer.
LOGYour journal. Every STATUS entry on one timeline, with weekly and monthly summaries. For a computer.
STYLEChanges how everything looks. For a computer. It cannot break anything.

You do not have to use all of them. STATUS and TRAIN are the two that matter.

Back up as well

The sheet is your copy off the device. A backup file is the one that puts everything back if something goes wrong.

  1. MAIN MENU, then DATA, then Back up everything.
  2. A file downloads. Keep it somewhere that is not the device: Telegram Saved Messages, Google Drive, or email it to yourself.

The DATA button shows a warning triangle once two weeks have passed since your last backup. When you see it, do those two steps.

Things that look broken but are not

  • It works with no signal. Every tap saves straight away, on the device, whether you have internet or not.
  • A new device starts empty. Paste your sync address into STATUS under DATA, sync, and your data arrives.
  • Screens start empty. There is nothing in here pretending to know how you should train or live. You fill it.
  • You can log any past day. Forgetting at the time is normal. Go back and put it in.
  • The sheet has odd tab names. That is how the apps talk to it. Read them all you like. Do not rename them.

What to send back

Anything at all, in whatever words come out. Most useful:

  • What you tried to do and could not work out how to do.
  • Anything that looked wrong, was slow, or made you stop and think.
  • A screenshot. Always worth more than a description.
  • What you stopped using after the first few days, and why, honestly.

Say which phone you have and which app you were in. A thing can be broken on one phone and fine on another.

If something goes wrong: your data is not inside the app, it is kept by your browser under the same web address. Closing the app, restarting the phone or reopening the link loses nothing. Clearing your browsing data does. If in doubt, make a backup first, then ask.
